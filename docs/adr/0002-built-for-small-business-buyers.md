# 2. Built for small business buyers of vendor contracts

Date: 2026-10-01. Status: Accepted.

The first version of Redline is built and checked for small business owners signing vendor, SaaS and service contracts. Severity ranking, default red lines and the labelled test set are tuned only for that user and that kind of document. Any document can still be uploaded and analysed. However, tenants, terms-of-service readers and freelancers are **not served**: nobody checks the output for their documents.

The research gave this segment the strongest evidence: real money lost to auto-renewal, notice windows and multi-year lock-ins, and an existing habit of paying lawyers $250–$750 per contract. Freelancers are the larger pool, but their biggest pain is nonpayment, which reading the contract does not prevent. There was no first-person tenant evidence. Terms-of-service readers are the least likely to pay. Proving trust across all four document types would multiply the test set without any evidence that it pays off.

## Consequences
- Redline gives up the largest audience (freelancers) and the "protect the little guy" story.
- An unserved reader gets output that nobody has checked for them, and the product must not suggest otherwise.
- Because the harm in this segment surfaces at renewal, not at signing, the summary pulls out every notice obligation, with its date and citation. There are no reminders, so Redline shows the deadline and acting on it remains the buyer's job.
